2 Software Requirements
2.4 Crossfeed Valve
2.4.1 Valve Commands
VCU-REQ-0010When VCU_OPER_MODE is (2) NORMAL and CC_XFEED_VALVE_CMD changes to (1) OPEN, the VCU shall report VCU_XFEED_VALVE_POS as (1) OPEN within 3 seconds.
VCU-REQ-0020When CC_XFEED_VALVE_CMD changes to (0) CLOSE, the VCU shall report VCU_XFEED_VALVE_POS as (0) CLOSED within 3 seconds.
VCU-REQ-0030In VCU_OPER_MODE (1) STANDBY the VCU shall not move the crossfeed valve: VCU_XFEED_VALVE_POS shall keep its last value regardless of CC_XFEED_VALVE_CMD.
2.4.2 Valve Monitoring
VCU-REQ-0040The VCU shall set VCU_VALVE_FAULT to (1) FAULT if VCU_XFEED_VALVE_POS does not reach the commanded position within 5 seconds.
VCU-REQ-0050The VCU shall transmit VCU_XFEED_VALVE_POS every 100 ms.
